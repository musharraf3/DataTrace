# Verified hackathon brief

Reviewed **October 28, 2026 Pacific** from the signed-in Cerebral Valley event and participant pages. This brief summarizes the event-specific material available at review time; it is not a record of accepted agreements or completed onboarding.

## Event, tracks, and themes

- Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon; Meta and Cerebral Valley; virtual worldwide event, subject to eligibility restrictions.
- Total anticipated cash prizes: **$1,000,000 USD**.
- **Indie Developer:** individual builders/students and eligible teams of up to four. Recommended track for DataTrace.
- **Startups:** technical founders building products; representative must be an authorized active employee.
- Enter one track and one project submission per project. Team representative submits for the group and identifies every contributor.
- **Build for Your World:** solve a domain problem understood firsthand. Recommended theme for DataTrace.
- **Push the Frontier:** apply Meta models to new capabilities in software, science, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials, or physical systems.

## Required project rules

1. Incorporate Meta models through Meta Model API and/or eligible Meta open-weight models. A coding-assistant-only workflow should not be assumed to satisfy the stage-one requirement that the submitted project reasonably use eligible Meta models.
2. Repository **must be public** and accessible to judges.
3. Present work built during the event. The demo must clearly identify the features, code, and functionality created during the hackathon. Failure to identify original contributions can disqualify the project.
4. Startups may build on existing codebases but must identify their new work. Use only code, data, and assets the team has rights to use; respect dependency licenses.
5. Solo entries are allowed. Maximum team size is four.
6. Submit an accessible **three-minute demo video**. Judges use the video, code, and live product as primary artifacts.
7. Late submissions are not considered. List all team members and appoint one authorized representative.

The public landing page still says official rules will be published before kickoff; the linked waiver currently contains Official Rules. Use the participant guide and waiver rather than assuming the landing-page placeholder means rules are unavailable.

## Anti-project examples

The guide warns against generic or policy-violating entries, including basic RAG applications, simple image analysis, generic education chatbots, medical-advice systems, job applicant screening, nutrition coaching, personality or sports analysis/coaching, and political/war projects. DataTrace should demonstrate an executing investigator and measured verification, rather than a generic SQL Q&A interface.

## Judging

Stage 1 is pass/fail: viable theme fit plus reasonable use of Meta Model API and/or Meta open-weight models in the event-built project.

Stage 2 scores each category from 0 to 10:

| Criterion | Weight | Implication for DataTrace |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Impact | 30% | Show a concrete analyst incident, who benefits, and measurable saved effort |
| Meta AI tooling | 25% | Model chooses tools and revises hypotheses from evidence |
| Technical depth and execution | 20% | Bounded execution, reproducible data, independent repair checks |
| Demo | 25% | Working live path and a clear three-minute video |

Stage 3: top five per track present through video demos in the live final round, using the same rubric. The earlier 50/50 idea/skill split was for application admission, not final project judging.

## Meta API account and $150 credits

**Account already exists**, confirmed by Musharraf Shaikh. Do not repeat account creation.

The claim form requires full name, the same email used for the accepted hackathon application, Meta Model API Team ID, and agreement to Meta API terms, acceptable-use policy, and hackathon credit terms. Team ID is visible in the signed-in dashboard URL as a path segment or `team_id` parameter.

- Each accepted participant may claim once for their own account; claims cannot be made for another participant.
- Claim deadline: November 4 at 9 PM ET / 6 PM PT.
- Form says credits are applied **within two business days**; announcements say they are distributed in batches.
- A payment method is required for an API call, even when using credits, according to the participant instructions. A temporary card preauthorization may appear and be released in a few business days.
- Verify credit balance on Billing. The guide calls its Spend Limit feature a **notification**; it does not establish a guaranteed hard cap.
- Credit request, payment setup, credit balance, and API-call success have not been verified in this review.
- The credit form was inspected without entering or submitting personal data or accepting terms.
- The linked **three-page Hackathon Credit Terms were reviewed visually**. They state that applied credits **do not expire**, remaining until used or account termination; use outside the hackathon is permitted under API terms. This is more specific than the waiver's ten-day development-window description.
- Credits are consumed before other payment methods. After they are exhausted, further API use is charged at standard rates to the account's payment method. The account owner is responsible for monitoring usage and excess charges.
- Credit claims and use are optional and do not affect participation or judging. Payment is not required to apply, participate, or submit; the hosted API service separately requires a valid payment method. Open-weight projects can compete without hosted API credit use.
- Credits are tied to one account, nontransferable, and have no cash value; duplicate accounts/claims or abuse can lead to denial or revocation. Unused credits are forfeited upon account/API-access termination. Meta may apply credits in batches and high demand can delay them beyond the target two-business-day period.
- Credit terms require eligible service geography, accepted-participant status, age 18+, account good standing, applicable sanctions/anti-corruption compliance, and authority under any employer rules governing things of value. Claim information is used to verify eligibility and apply credits. The version effective when the claim is accepted governs that grant.

## Official Rules and waiver summary

The rules require accepted participants aged 18+ and of local majority age, with location/sanctions and organizer-employment/household exclusions. Projects need authorized assets and original contributions; eligible licensed open-source components are permitted. Participants retain submission ownership but grant organizers broad perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free rights to use and adapt materials. Event materials are not confidential. The waiver also addresses recording/publicity, marketing data sharing, releases, indemnities, and California/San Francisco governing-law and venue terms.

Prizes require eligibility verification, signed rules/waiver, and applicable tax documentation. Cerebral Valley administers USD payment through a payout provider (currently Tremendous). Teams specify allocation in writing. Winners bear applicable taxes and fees. Payment is described as within 30 days after verification and required documents, with funds released after receipt from Meta. Rules may change. Read the full waiver for the governing text; this document does not determine individual legal/tax eligibility or record agreement acceptance.
